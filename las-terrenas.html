The short version

How to get to Las Terrenas, in 30 seconds

Las Terrenas is a beach town on the north side of the Samaná peninsula, in Samaná province — a French- and Italian-inflected place of beach restaurants, long sandy bays and forested hills behind. Most visitors arrive through Puerto Plata (POP) or Santo Domingo (SDQ) and cross by road. Once you are here

What is within reach of Las Terrenas

Las Terrenas is a working beach town with a French and Italian streak, strung along the sand rather than built around a centre. These are the places our guests actually ask us to take them to.

Playa Bonita

Town beach

The beach just west of the town: a curve of pale sand with small hotels and restaurants set back under the palms. Calm enough to swim, big enough to walk, and the sunsets are the reason people book a second week.

Playa Cosón

Long wild beach

The big one, further west again: an enormous stretch of sand with coconut palms behind it and almost nothing built on it. A handful of beach restaurants, and space enough that you can walk until the town is out of sight.

Playa Las Ballenas

In town

The town's own beach, named after the little islands off it. Shallow, protected and lined with the restaurants of the Pueblo de los Pescadores — the old fishing village that became the place everyone eats.

El Limón waterfall

Inland excursion

A tall waterfall in the hills between Las Terrenas and Samaná town, falling into a pool you can swim in. The usual way up is on horseback along a muddy track, which is half the point.

Samaná town & the bay

Across the peninsula

Over the hills on the south side: a harbour town on a wide bay, with boats out to Cayo Levantado. The bay is the one humpback whales are known for, and in the first months of the year it is the reason a lot of people come to this peninsula at all.

Cayo Levantado

Island in the bay

A small island in Samaná Bay reached by boat from Samaná town, with a public beach at one end and a resort at the other. The standard boat trip from this side of the peninsula.
