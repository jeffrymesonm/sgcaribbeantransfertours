The short version

How to get to Cofresí, in 30 seconds

Cofresí is a resort and villa area on the coast just west of Puerto Plata city, in Puerto Plata province. It is best known as the home of Ocean World Adventure Park, and it shares this stretch of coast with Costambar, a quieter residential neighbourhood with its own calm beach. Both are covered by the same fare. SG Caribbean Transfers & Tours runs private door-to-door transfers from Gregorio Luperón airport (POP) to Cofresí and Costambar at a fixed US$48.50 for 1 to 6 guests, with no prepayment: you pay your driver in cash when you arrive.

Arriving by cruise ship

Amber Cove is the closest pier of all

Amber Cove sits in the next bay along from Cofresí, which makes this the shortest pier-to-beach run on the whole coast — and the easiest half-day if your ship docks there.

Taino Bay → Cofresí

In Puerto Plata city

Taino Bay is in Puerto Plata itself, so the drive to Cofresí goes west out of the city along the coast road. We meet you outside the terminal gate.

Amber Cove → Cofresí

The neighbouring bay

Amber Cove is in Maimón, the bay immediately west of Cofresí. If your ship berths here, Ocean World and the Cofresí beach are the nearest things on the coast to your gangway.

The all-aboard maths, done for you

A typical Puerto Plata port call lasts 8 to 9 hours. From Amber Cove this is the shortest run we do, so almost the whole call is yours on the ground — enough for Ocean World and still an unhurried lunch. We plan every cruise day backwards from your all-aboard time and return you to the pier with a margin, never on the dot.

Once you are here

What is within reach of Cofresí

Cofresí is small and mostly residential once you step off the resort strip. Most of what there is to do sits either on its own beach or a short way back towards Puerto Plata.

Ocean World Adventure Park

Marine park

The reason most people have heard of Cofresí: a marine park on the beachfront with dolphin, sea lion and shark encounters, a bird sanctuary and a rainforest walk. It is the standard family day out on this coast, and it is right here rather than an excursion away.

Playa Cofresí

In town

A small cove with the resorts behind it and a handful of beach bars on the sand. Not the biggest beach on the coast, but sheltered, easy to swim from and about thirty seconds from wherever you are staying.

Costambar

Neighbouring beach

The residential neighbour: villas, condos and a long, shallow, very calm beach that fills with Dominican families at weekends. Quieter than Cofresí and a favourite with long-stay visitors and snowbirds.

Puerto Plata & the cable car

Neighbouring city

The city next door, back east along the coast road: Fortaleza San Felipe, the Victorian centre, the Malecón and the cable car up Pico Isabel de Torres. See the full Puerto Plata guide.

Playa Dorada

Resort beach

The other resort cluster on this bay, on the far side of Puerto Plata, with a golf course in the middle of it. Worth knowing about mainly so you do not confuse the two when you book a hotel.

Charcos de Damajagua

Full-day excursion

Limestone pools you climb up and then jump and slide back down, inland from this stretch of coast. The single most requested excursion from any of the resorts here.
