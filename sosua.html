The short version

How to get to Sosúa, in 30 seconds

Sosúa is a beach town on the north coast of the Dominican Republic, in the province of Puerto Plata. The closest airport is Gregorio Luperón International (POP), roughly 19 km away — about a 25-minute drive. SG Caribbean Transfers & Tours runs private door-to-door transfers from POP to Sosúa at a fixed US$23.95 for 1 to 6 guests, with no prepayment: you pay your driver in cash when you arrive.

Arriving by cruise ship

Sosúa is an easy half-day from either pier

Both of Puerto Plata's cruise terminals are within an hour of Sosúa, which makes the beach a comfortable independent excursion — not a rush.

Taino Bay → Sosúa

~27 km · ~35 min

Taino Bay sits right in Puerto Plata city, so you are on the coastal highway within minutes of stepping off the ship. We meet you outside the terminal gate.

Amber Cove → Sosúa

~32 km · ~40–45 min

Amber Cove is in Maimón, just west of Puerto Plata. The drive to Sosúa follows the same coast road, past Playa Dorada and the airport turn-off.

The all-aboard maths, done for you

A typical Puerto Plata port call lasts 8 to 9 hours. A round trip to Sosúa uses roughly 1 h 15 of that in driving, which still leaves you six hours or more on the ground. We plan every cruise day backwards from your ship's all-aboard time and return you to the pier with a margin, never on the dot.

Meet the drivers

Once you are here

What is within reach of Sosúa

Sosúa is small enough to walk and central enough to use as a base. These are the places our guests actually ask us to drive them to.

Playa Sosúa

In town

The town's main bay: a horseshoe of calm, reef-sheltered water lined with beach restaurants. Snorkelling starts a few metres from the sand, which is why Sosúa is where most people on this coast learn to dive.

Playa Alicia

In town

A quieter cliff-backed beach a short walk from the centre. Fewer vendors, better mornings, and the easiest swim in Sosúa if you want the water to yourself.

Sosúa Jewish Museum

In town

Sosúa exists in its modern form because in 1940 the Dominican Republic took in Jewish refugees fleeing Europe and settled them here. The small museum and the wooden synagogue beside it tell that story — the most surprising half hour in town.

Cabarete

~8 km · ~15 min

The kitesurfing capital of the Caribbean, and the closest place to go out at night. Kite Beach in the afternoon wind is worth the drive even if you never leave the sand.

Puerto Plata & the cable car

~25 km · ~30 min

The teleférico climbs Pico Isabel de Torres for the view over the whole bay. Add Fort San Felipe, the Amber Museum and the Victorian houses of the old centre and it is a comfortable half day.

27 Charcos de Damajagua

~45 min

Twenty-seven limestone pools you climb up and then jump and slide back down. The single most requested excursion from Sosúa, and the one people talk about for years afterwards.
